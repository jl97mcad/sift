name: hand-off
description: Choosing who does a task, wording it, and following it up
---
- Read first. If a lookup (sessions, read_session, read_source, paseo logs) answers it, answer yourself.
- Pick the session whose description in your instructions fits: usually Control for doing things (PRs, deploys, the board, starting or fixing work), and a plan's lead, with its plan number, for one plan's detail.
- Write the task in their words, plus anything from the conversation it needs: names, plan numbers, what "that" refers to.
- Say who you've asked in a few words, then stop. The result is spoken when it comes back; job_status tells you where it's up to.
- Never say it's done before the reply says so. Sends outside the company are proposals the person approves.

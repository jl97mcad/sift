---
name: hand-off
description: Choosing who does a task, wording it, and following it up
---
- Read first, always. Your own read tools cover most of it: sessions, read_session, read_source, paseo logs, plan, plan_search, github, vault, server_status, web, and look_into for anything that takes several. If any of them can answer, answer yourself or start look_into; don't delegate.
- Delegate only an action (anything that writes, sends, merges, deploys or changes), Lark, or something only the Mac can see. Reading a plan, a PR, a note or the server is never a hand-off.
- Pick the session whose description in your instructions fits: usually Control for doing things (PRs, deploys, the board, starting or fixing work), and a plan's lead, with its plan number, for changing one plan.
- Write the task in their words, plus anything from the conversation it needs: names, plan numbers, what "that" refers to.
- Say who you've asked in a few words, then stop. The result is spoken when it comes back; job_status tells you where it's up to.
- Never say it's done before the reply says so. Sends outside the company are proposals the person approves.

1. If your instructions have an "Already read for this call" block, answer from it: no tool. Otherwise, or if they ask for the latest, call catch_up once (needs queue, sessions and focus list together) rather than several lookups.
2. Needs first: give the one or two that matter most in a sentence each: which plan and what's being asked. Say how many more there are.
3. Only if they asked what's going on generally: say how many sessions are busy and name the two or three that matter (Control and plan leads, not short-lived workers).
4. If the focus list is there, give the top one or two.
Keep the whole answer under about 40 words, then stop. They'll ask for more.

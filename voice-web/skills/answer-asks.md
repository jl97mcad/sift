---
name: answer-asks
description: "Yes to that", "tell them no", "answer JON-123", "yes to both": recording the person's own answer to an open ask
---
Only when answer_ask is offered, and only for the person's own spoken answer to an open ask in the needs queue.

1. Find the ask: the plan number from what they said or from the ask you just read them. If you're not sure which ask they mean, ask; never guess.
2. Call answer_ask with the plan, their exact words (verbatim, not tidied up or paraphrased) and confirmed false. Nothing is written.
3. If it says the plan has several open asks, read the short list and ask which; call again with `which`.
4. Speak the read-back it gives you: the ask, their words, and what a yes lets happen. Then stop and wait.
5. Only after a clear yes to that read-back ("yes", "go ahead", "record it"), call again with the same plan and words and confirmed true. A "yeah" while talking about something else is not a yes.
6. Say what came back: recorded and no longer open, or that it failed. If it failed, say so and stop: never retry it yourself.

- Sends outside the company, money, access, signatures and production writes: the read-back names what will happen ("this lets Control send the message to the partner team") and needs a clear yes.
- "Yes to both" or "yes to all": one answer_ask per ask, each read back and each confirmed.
- Never infer an answer from tone or from the recommendation, and never answer for anyone else.
- In a dry run nothing is written; say it was a test.
